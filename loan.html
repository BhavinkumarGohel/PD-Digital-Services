Basic Eligibility Criteria

Standard profile criteria across partner banks for fast sanctioning.

Age Bracket

Minimum 21 years at application time; up to 65 years at loan maturity.

CIBIL / Credit Score

Score of 700+ preferred for best interest rates and minimal processing charges.

Income Stability

Min. 1-2 years continuous employment (Salaried) or 2-3 years business operations with filed ITRs.

Geographic Area

Available for properties and registered applicants throughout Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, and Gujarat.

Required Documents for Loan Processing

Keep these documents ready for verification and doorstep pickup

Salaried Individuals
  • PAN Card & Identity Proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID)
  • Current Residence Proof (Electricity bill / Rent agreement)
  • Latest 3 Months Salary Slips
  • Latest 6 Months Salary Account Bank Statement
  • Latest Form 16 (2 Years)
Self-Employed / Business
  • PAN Card & Identity Proof of Promoters
  • Business Registration (GST Certificate / Gumastadhara)
  • Last 2-3 Years ITR with Computation, Balance Sheet & P&L
  • Last 12 Months Current & Savings Bank Statements
  • Existing Loan Sanction Letters & Track Records
Property Papers (For Home & LAP)
  • Complete Chain of Title Documents (Sale Deed)
  • Approved Map / Sanction Plan from Local Authority
  • Latest Property Tax Bill & Payment Receipt
  • Allotment Letter / Builder Agreement (For New Purchase)
  • Foreclosure Letter & LOD (For Balance Transfer)
