Required Documents for Smooth Processing

Keep soft copies or physical copies ready for prompt document verification

For Income Tax Return (ITR)
  • PAN Card & Linked Aadhaar Number
  • Form 16 Part A & B (For Salaried)
  • Bank Statements (All active savings/current accounts)
  • Investment Proofs (PPF, ELSS, Insurance receipts)
  • Stock Broker Annual Capital Gain Statement (if applicable)
For GST Registration
  • PAN Card of Proprietor / Partners / Directors
  • Aadhaar Card (Must be mobile-linked for OTP)
  • Business Electricity Bill (Latest 2 months)
  • Rent Agreement & NOC from Landlord (if rented)
  • Cancelled Cheque / Bank Statement Header

  • Existing PAN Card Copy (or Allotment Letter)
  • Aadhaar Card (Supporting correct name / DOB)
  • 2 Passport Size Photographs (White background)
  • Birth Certificate / 10th Marks Card (for DOB dispute)
  • Marriage Certificate / Gazette (for surname change)
